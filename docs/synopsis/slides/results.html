Smriti · 10

These numbers come from smriti eval with the offline extractive reasoner on 23 QA and 11 red-team items. They show the pipeline works: System 1 handles about a third of questions alone, guardrails block every direct attack with no false refusals, and no PII leaks. The latency gap between the System 1 fast path and the local System 2 model is the motivation for escalation. LLM-based results on a larger held-out set come next.
