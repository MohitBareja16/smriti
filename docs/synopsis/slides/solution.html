The idea

One assistant that knows you, and only you

Study

“Explain deadlock from my OS notes” answers with the page number.

Ask

“What was my CGPA in semester 5?” gets an answer plus the source document.

Fetch

“Give me my AWS certificate” finds the file in your encrypted vault.

Track

Upcoming exams, certification expiries and application deadlines.

Tailor

Paste a job description to get a match score and a tailored resume draft.

The AI drafts, and you approve. It never submits or posts on its own.

Personal Agentic RAG · 3

Five capabilities: study, ask, fetch, track and tailor. The human-in-the-loop rule is key: anything that changes the outside world, like a LinkedIn post or a job application, is only drafted, and the user submits it.
